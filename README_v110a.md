# CYTOPLASM v1.1a — Living Matter
Visual update of CYTOPLASM v1.0, with full 3-minute micro-world level intact.

- Organic wobbly transparent-but-cloudy cytoplasm; moving internal vesicles and filaments.
- The playable cell has a clearly visible pulsing nucleus, smooth mass reduction, elastic jump/landing/recoil animations.
- Every gel reacts visually to landing, formation and projectile hits; the collision shapes and physics remain unchanged.
- Core gameplay preserved: A/D movement, W jump, left click shoots, R detonates, N restarts. Bio charges, Bio Surge, enemies and checkpoints unchanged.
- Lab preview: docs/material-lab.html; full playable: docs/living-matter.html.
- Removing eyes from bacteria and viruses, and adding biological multilayer parallax, are planned for v1.1b rather than v1.1a.

Browser and engine regression tests passed; GDevelop Preview itself still needs manual testing.
